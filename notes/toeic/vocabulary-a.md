<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="abreast">
<h2 id="abreast">abreast <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adv.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 並排；掌握最新情況

### Collocations & patterns

- walk / stand / ride abreast
- keep / stay / be abreast of developments

### Example

We hold weekly meetings to keep abreast of project developments.

### Usage note

Use **abreast of** for being up to date; **abreast** alone can mean side by side.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="activate">
<h2 id="activate">activate <span class="vocabulary-pos">[v.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 啟用；使運作；激活

### Collocations & patterns

- activate a device / service / feature / response
- activate an account
- activate the alarm
- activate muscles

### Example

Please activate your account using the link in the email.

### Usage note

**Activate the alarm** means make it active or trigger it, depending on context; **set off the alarm** clearly means trigger it.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="adapter">
<h2 id="adapter">adapter <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 轉接器；變壓器；改編者

### Collocations & patterns

- power / travel / AC / plug / network adapter
- adapter cable
- film adapter

### Example

You will need a travel adapter to charge your laptop abroad.

### Usage note

Also spelled **adaptor**. A **film adapter** can mean a person adapting material for film, or equipment in a film-related context. The resulting movie is a **film adaptation**, not an adapter. [Dictionary reference](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/adapter).

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="adhere">
<h2 id="adhere">adhere <span class="vocabulary-pos">[v.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 遵守；遵循；黏附

### Collocations & patterns

- adhere to the rules / the law
- adhere to a policy / schedule
- adhere to tradition / principles

### Example

All contractors must adhere to the safety rules.

### Usage note

When the meaning is follow or obey, use **adhere to**, not adhere alone before the rule.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="adjacent">
<h2 id="adjacent">adjacent <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 相鄰的；毗連的

### Collocations & patterns

- adjacent to
- adjacent building / room / area / street / property / seat

### Example

The training room is adjacent to the main office.

### Usage note

Use **adjacent to + noun** after the verb, or **adjacent + noun** before the noun.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="advancement">
<h2 id="advancement">advancement <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 進步；發展；晉升

### Collocations & patterns

- career / technological / social advancement
- professional / rapid / academic advancement

### Example

The company provides training opportunities for career advancement.

### Usage note

**Career advancement** usually concerns professional progress or promotion.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="advisable">
<h2 id="advisable">advisable <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 明智的；可取的

### Collocations & patterns

- advisable to check
- seem advisable
- an advisable course of action
- an advisable precaution
- hardly / financially / medically advisable

### Example

It is advisable to check the train schedule before leaving.

### Usage note

Common pattern: **It is advisable to + verb**. **Hardly advisable** means generally unwise.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="advisor">
<h2 id="advisor">advisor <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 顧問；指導者

### Collocations & patterns

- academic / financial / legal / senior / trusted advisor
- serve as an advisor
- consult an advisor

### Example

Consult a financial advisor before making a major investment.

### Usage note

**Adviser** is another standard spelling. Use **serve as an advisor**, with **as**.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="advisory">
<h2 id="advisory">advisory <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.; n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 顧問的；諮詢的；公告／警報

### Collocations & patterns

- advisory board / role / committee / services / opinion
- travel / weather advisory

### Example

The weather advisory warned travelers of heavy snow.

### Usage note

An **advisor** is a person; **advisory** modifies a noun or denotes an official notice.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="affirmative">
<h2 id="affirmative">affirmative <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.; n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 肯定的；贊成的；肯定答覆

### Collocations & patterns

- affirmative answer / response / action / vote / statement / case
- in the affirmative

### Example

The committee answered the proposal in the affirmative.

### Usage note

**In the affirmative** means yes, in formal language. **Affirmative case** is mainly debate terminology; **affirmative action** has a distinct policy meaning.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="affordable">
<h2 id="affordable">affordable <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 負擔得起的

### Collocations & patterns

- an affordable price
- affordable housing / rent / option
- more affordable
- affordable for everyone

### Example

The hotel offers comfortable rooms at an affordable price.

### Usage note

**Affordable for + person/group** identifies who can pay; **affordable housing** is a common phrase.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="afterward">
<h2 id="afterward">afterward <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adv.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 之後；後來

### Collocations & patterns

- shortly / soon / immediately / not long afterward
- do something afterward
- the day afterward

### Example

The presentation ends at noon, and lunch will be served shortly afterward.

### Usage note

**Afterwards** is another form. **Something afterward** alone is not a fixed collocation; use it within a phrase such as **do something afterward**. [Dictionary reference](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/afterwards).

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="air-force">
<h2 id="air-force">air force <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 空軍

### Collocations & patterns

- join / serve in the air force
- an air force base / academy
- an air force pilot / commander / training program

### Example

She served in the air force before becoming a commercial pilot.

### Usage note

Use **serve in the air force**. Capitalize when it is part of an official name, such as the **United States Air Force**.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="airfare">
<h2 id="airfare">airfare <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 機票票價

### Collocations & patterns

- cheap / round-trip airfare
- airfare prices / an airfare deal
- pay / cover the airfare
- an airfare increase

### Example

The company will cover the airfare for the business trip.

### Usage note

**Cover the airfare** means pay the flight cost. **Airfare prices** is understandable, though **airfares** is often enough.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="alternatively">
<h2 id="alternatively">alternatively <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adv.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 或者；作為另一選擇

### Collocations & patterns

- or alternatively
- alternatively, we could ...

### Example

We could meet on Monday. Alternatively, we could discuss it by email.

### Usage note

Use to introduce a different option. A comma commonly follows it at the beginning of a sentence.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="amenity">
<h2 id="amenity">amenity <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 便利設施；生活設施

### Collocations & patterns

- local / public / basic / modern amenities
- hotel / residential / community amenities

### Example

The hotel amenities include a gym and a swimming pool.

### Usage note

Often plural: **amenities**. A single facility can be **an amenity**.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="anew">
<h2 id="anew">anew <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adv.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 重新；再度

### Collocations & patterns

- start / begin / live / see / build / create anew

### Example

After reviewing the feedback, the team decided to start anew.

### Usage note

Often follows the verb. **See something anew** means see it in a fresh way; some combinations are more literary than everyday business English.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="announcer">
<h2 id="announcer">announcer <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 播音員；廣播員

### Collocations & patterns

- radio / TV / sports / news / flight / stadium announcer
- work as an announcer

### Example

The stadium announcer introduced the players before the game.

### Usage note

For someone reading television news, **news anchor** or **newsreader** may be more specific.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="anytime">
<h2 id="anytime">anytime <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adv.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 隨時；任何時候

### Collocations & patterns

- anytime soon / now / at all / you want
- come anytime
- call the helpline anytime

### Example

You can contact the support team anytime you need help.

### Usage note

Use **at any time** after **at**. **Any time** is also written as two words, especially when time is a noun phrase.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="appraisal">
<h2 id="appraisal">appraisal <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 評估；考核；估價

### Collocations & patterns

- performance / employee / formal / annual appraisal
- property / real estate / critical appraisal

### Example

Employees receive an annual performance appraisal in December.

### Usage note

Spelling: **employee**, not empolyee. **Critical appraisal** means careful evaluation, not necessarily a negative judgment.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="apprehensive">
<h2 id="apprehensive">apprehensive <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 憂慮的；不安的

### Collocations & patterns

- feel / be apprehensive about
- be apprehensive of
- look apprehensive
- apprehensive at the thought of ...
- slightly / visibly apprehensive

### Example

She felt apprehensive about giving her first presentation.

### Usage note

Use **about + noun / -ing**. Complete **at the thought** with **of + noun / -ing**.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="arc">
<h2 id="arc">arc <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.; v.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 弧線；電弧；沿弧線移動

### Collocations & patterns

- an arc of light
- in an arc
- describe an arc
- arc across
- arc through the air
- electric arc / arc lamp

### Example

The ball arced through the air and landed near the gate.

### Usage note

Spelling: **across**, not accross. Verb forms include **arced** and **arcing**. **Describe an arc** means trace out a curved path.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="architectural">
<h2 id="architectural">architectural <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 建築的；建築學的

### Collocations & patterns

- architectural design / style / features / heritage
- architectural firm / drawing / project

### Example

The renovation will preserve the building’s original architectural features.

### Usage note

Related noun: **architecture**; a person who designs buildings is an **architect**.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="archive">
<h2 id="archive">archive <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.; v.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 檔案庫；檔案；歸檔

### Collocations & patterns

- digital / public archive
- archive records / documents / material
- historical / news archive

### Example

Please archive the completed project documents at the end of the month.

### Usage note

As a verb, **archive** takes the material directly. The plural **archives** is also common for a collection or institution.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="attendee">
<h2 id="attendee">attendee <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 出席者；與會者

### Collocations & patterns

- conference / event / registered attendee
- attendee list / badge / feedback
- attendee numbers / the number of attendees

### Example

Each registered attendee will receive a badge at the entrance.

### Usage note

Use **the number of attendees** for a count. **Attendee number** can instead mean an identifying number assigned to one attendee.

</section>
