<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="abreast">
<h2 id="abreast">abreast <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adv.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 並排；掌握最新情況

### Collocations & patterns

- walk / stand / ride abreast
- keep / stay / be abreast of developments

### Example

We hold weekly meetings to keep abreast of project developments.

### Usage note

Use **abreast of** for being up to date; **abreast** alone can mean side by side.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="activate">
<h2 id="activate">activate <span class="vocabulary-pos">[v.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 啟用；使運作；激活

### Collocations & patterns

- activate a device / service / feature / response
- activate an account
- activate the alarm
- activate muscles

### Example

Please activate your account using the link in the email.

### Usage note

**Activate the alarm** means make it active or trigger it, depending on context; **set off the alarm** clearly means trigger it.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="adapter">
<h2 id="adapter">adapter <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 轉接器；變壓器；改編者

### Collocations & patterns

- power / travel / AC / plug / network adapter
- adapter cable
- film adapter

### Example

You will need a travel adapter to charge your laptop abroad.

### Usage note

Also spelled **adaptor**. A **film adapter** can mean a person adapting material for film, or equipment in a film-related context. The resulting movie is a **film adaptation**, not an adapter. [Dictionary reference](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/adapter).

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="adhere">
<h2 id="adhere">adhere <span class="vocabulary-pos">[v.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 遵守；遵循；黏附

### Collocations & patterns

- adhere to the rules / the law
- adhere to a policy / schedule
- adhere to tradition / principles

### Example

All contractors must adhere to the safety rules.

### Usage note

When the meaning is follow or obey, use **adhere to**, not adhere alone before the rule.

</section>

### Collocations & patterns

- adjacent to
- adjacent building / room / area / street / property / seat

### Example

The training room is adjacent to the main office.

Use **adjacent to + noun** after the verb, or **adjacent + noun** before the noun.

</section>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="advisable">
<h2 id="advisable">advisable <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 明智的；可取的

### Collocations & patterns

- advisable to check
- seem advisable
- an advisable course of action
- an advisable precaution
- hardly / financially / medically advisable

### Example

It is advisable to check the train schedule before leaving.

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="advisor">
<h2 id="advisor">advisor <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 顧問；指導者

### Collocations & patterns

- academic / financial / legal / senior / trusted advisor
- serve as an advisor
- consult an advisor

### Example

Consult a financial advisor before making a major investment.

### Usage note

**Adviser** is another standard spelling. Use **serve as an advisor**, with **as**.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="advisory">
<h2 id="advisory">advisory <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.; n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 顧問的；諮詢的；公告／警報

### Collocations & patterns

- advisory board / role / committee / services / opinion
- travel / weather advisory

### Example

The weather advisory warned travelers of heavy snow.

### Usage note

An **advisor** is a person; **advisory** modifies a noun or denotes an official notice.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="affirmative">
<h2 id="affirmative">affirmative <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.; n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 肯定的；贊成的；肯定答覆

### Collocations & patterns

- affirmative answer / response / action / vote / statement / case
- in the affirmative

### Example

The committee answered the proposal in the affirmative.

### Usage note

**In the affirmative** means yes, in formal language. **Affirmative case** is mainly debate terminology; **affirmative action** has a distinct policy meaning.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="affordable">
<h2 id="affordable">affordable <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 負擔得起的

### Collocations & patterns

- an affordable price
- affordable housing / rent / option
- more affordable
- affordable for everyone

### Example

The hotel offers comfortable rooms at an affordable price.

**Affordable for + person/group** identifies who can pay; **affordable housing** is a common phrase.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="afterward">
<h2 id="afterward">afterward <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adv.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 之後；後來

### Collocations & patterns

- shortly / soon / immediately / not long afterward
- do something afterward
- the day afterward

### Example

The presentation ends at noon, and lunch will be served shortly afterward.

### Usage note

**Afterwards** is another form. **Something afterward** alone is not a fixed collocation; use it within a phrase such as **do something afterward**. [Dictionary reference](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/afterwards).

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="air-force">
<h2 id="air-force">air force <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 空軍

### Collocations & patterns

- join / serve in the air force
- an air force base / academy
- an air force pilot / commander / training program

### Example

She served in the air force before becoming a commercial pilot.

Use **serve in the air force**. Capitalize when it is part of an official name, such as the **United States Air Force**.

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="airfare">
<h2 id="airfare">airfare <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** 機票票價

### Collocations & patterns

- cheap / round-trip airfare
- airfare prices / an airfare deal
- pay / cover the airfare
- an airfare increase

### Example

The company will cover the airfare for the business trip.

**Cover the airfare** means pay the flight cost. **Airfare prices** is understandable, though **airfares** is often enough.

### Collocations & patterns

- or alternatively

### Example

We could meet on Monday. Alternatively, we could discuss it by email.

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="anew">

### Collocations & patterns

- feel / be apprehensive about
- be apprehensive of
- look apprehensive
- apprehensive at the thought of ...

### Example

She felt apprehensive about giving her first presentation.

**Meaning:** 弧線；電弧；沿弧線移動

### Collocations & patterns

- an arc of light
- in an arc
- describe an arc
- arc across
- arc through the air
- electric arc / arc lamp

### Example

The ball arced through the air and landed near the gate.

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="audit">
<h2 id="audit">audit <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.; vt.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">審計；查核；審核帳目；旁聽課程</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- conduct an audit
- an internal / financial / tax audit
- an audit report
- audit a company’s accounts
- audit a class / course
- an auditing student

### Example

An independent firm will conduct a financial audit next month.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">一家獨立事務所將於下個月進行財務審計。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Tax audit</strong> 是稅務查核。<strong>Audit a class</strong> 是「旁聽課程，通常不取得學分」；旁聽生可說 <strong>an auditing student</strong> 或 <strong>an auditor</strong>。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="audition">
<h2 id="audition">audition <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.; vi.; vt.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">試鏡；試演；參加試鏡；為表演者試鏡</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- go to / go for an audition
- audition for a role
- hold auditions
- pass / fail an audition
- an audition tape
- an open audition

### Example

The theater will hold open auditions for the lead role on Saturday.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">劇院將於週六為主角舉辦公開試鏡。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Audition</strong> 也能當動詞：<strong>audition for a role</strong> 是「為某角色參加試鏡」；<strong>audition actors</strong> 是「對演員進行試鏡」。名詞可用 <strong>hold auditions</strong> 表示舉辦試鏡。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="auditor">
<h2 id="auditor">auditor <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">審計人員；查核人員；旁聽生</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- an internal / external / independent auditor
- a lead / government / qualified auditor
- an auditor’s report

### Example

The external auditor requested copies of the expense records.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">外部審計人員要求提供費用紀錄的副本。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant">在財務情境中是「審計人員」，在課堂情境也可能是「旁聽生」。<strong>Auditor’s report</strong> 指審計人員出具的報告；注意 <strong>auditor</strong> 與 <strong>audition</strong> 的意思不同。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="authorization">
<h2 id="authorization">authorization <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">授權；批准；許可</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- give / grant authorization
- obtain / require authorization
- written / prior authorization
- without authorization
- authorization to access records

### Example

You must obtain written authorization before accessing these records.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">存取這些紀錄前，必須先取得書面授權。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant">常當不可數名詞使用：<strong>obtain authorization</strong>、<strong>without authorization</strong>。<strong>Prior authorization</strong> 是事前許可；動詞為 <strong>authorize</strong>，如 authorize someone to do something。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="auto">
<h2 id="auto">auto <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.; attributive]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">汽車（較口語）；用在名詞前表示與汽車有關</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- the auto industry
- auto insurance / repair / parts
- an auto show / dealer / loan

### Example

The bank offers auto loans to eligible customers.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">這家銀行向符合資格的客戶提供汽車貸款。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Auto</strong> 作名詞主要見於美式英語，表示 automobile；auto industry 等為名詞前的修飾用法。正式描述汽車相關技術時，也常用形容詞 <strong>automotive</strong>。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="automate">
<h2 id="automate">automate <span class="vocabulary-pos">[vt.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">使自動化</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- automate a process / tasks / production
- automate repetitive work
- automate the system
- fully automate production

### Example

The company plans to automate repetitive data-entry tasks.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">公司計畫將重複性的資料輸入工作自動化。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Automate</strong> 是動詞；<strong>automated</strong> 表示已自動化的，如 an automated system；<strong>automation</strong> 是「自動化」這個名詞。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="automotive">
<h2 id="automotive">automotive <span class="vocabulary-pos">[adj.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">汽車的；汽車產業或技術相關的</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- the automotive industry / market
- automotive technology / parts / design
- an automotive engineer
- automotive manufacturing

### Example

The factory supplies parts to the automotive industry.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">這家工廠供應汽車產業所需的零件。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Automotive</strong> 是形容詞，修飾與汽車有關的事物；<strong>automate</strong> 則是「使自動化」，兩者不能因字形相近就混用。</p>

</section>
