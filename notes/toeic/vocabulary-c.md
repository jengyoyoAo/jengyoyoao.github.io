<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="cab">
<h2 id="cab">cab <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">計程車；卡車或機械的駕駛室</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- take / call / hail / share a cab
- a cab driver / fare
- in the cab

### Example

We shared a cab from the station to the hotel.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">我們從車站共乘計程車前往飯店。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Hail a cab</strong> 是招手攔計程車；<strong>call a cab</strong> 是叫車。<strong>Cab</strong> 在卡車或工程機械的情境中也可指駕駛室。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="caller">
<h2 id="caller">caller <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">打電話來的人；來電者；來訪者</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- the first caller
- an anonymous / frequent caller
- screen callers
- caller ID
- a sales caller
- a welcome caller

### Example

The receptionist screens callers before transferring their calls.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">接待人員會先確認來電者的身分或來意，再轉接電話。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Screen callers</strong> 是篩選、確認來電者；<strong>caller ID</strong> 是來電顯示。<strong>A welcome caller</strong> 可依情境表示受歡迎的來電者或訪客，並非固定職稱。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="cancellation">
<h2 id="cancellation">cancellation <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">取消；撤銷</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- flight / order / ticket / reservation cancellation
- policy / service cancellation
- a cancellation notice
- a cancellation fee

### Example

Please read the cancellation policy before making a reservation.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">訂位前，請先閱讀取消政策。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant">注意 <strong>a cancellation policy</strong> 是「取消規定」，而 <strong>policy cancellation</strong> 可指「保單取消」；詞序影響意思。此字也見美式拼法 cancelation。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="capitol">
<h2 id="capitol">Capitol <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">美國國會大廈；州議會大廈</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- the Capitol
- visit / tour the Capitol
- inside / outside / near the Capitol
- the Capitol building
- a state capitol

### Example

Visitors can join a guided tour of the Capitol.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">遊客可以參加國會大廈的導覽。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>The Capitol</strong> 大寫時通常指美國華盛頓的國會大廈；<strong>a state capitol</strong> 指州議會大廈。不要和 <strong>capital</strong>（首都、資本、大寫字母）混淆。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="cartridge">
<h2 id="cartridge">cartridge <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">可替換的匣式容器；墨水匣、碳粉匣；彈藥</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- an ink / toner / printer cartridge
- a film / tape cartridge
- a fountain pen cartridge
- a cartridge case / belt

### Example

The printer needs a new toner cartridge.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">這台印表機需要更換新的碳粉匣。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant">辦公室常見 <strong>ink cartridge</strong>（墨水匣）與 <strong>toner cartridge</strong>（碳粉匣）。<strong>Cartridge case</strong>、<strong>cartridge belt</strong> 則屬彈藥情境，分別指彈殼與彈帶。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="caterer">
<h2 id="caterer">caterer <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">外燴業者；提供餐飲承辦服務的人或公司</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- hire a caterer
- a wedding / corporate caterer
- a local / professional / recommended caterer

### Example

We hired a local caterer for the annual company dinner.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">我們聘請當地外燴業者承辦公司年度晚宴。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Caterer</strong> 指提供餐飲服務的業者；服務本身是 <strong>catering</strong>。動詞可說 <strong>cater for an event</strong> 或 <strong>cater an event</strong>。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="certification">
<h2 id="certification">certification <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">認證；資格認定；認證程序或狀態</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- professional / safety certification
- food safety certification
- the certification process / exam
- a certification body
- obtain certification

### Example

Applicants must obtain food safety certification before starting work.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">申請人必須先取得食品安全認證，才能開始工作。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Certification</strong> 偏重認證程序、資格或狀態；<strong>a certificate</strong> 是證明文件或證書。<strong>Certification body</strong> 指辦理認證的機構。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="chairperson">
<h2 id="chairperson">chairperson <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">主席；會議或委員會主持人</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- chairperson of the board / committee
- elect / appoint a chairperson
- serve as a chairperson
- chairperson of the meeting
- vice chairperson

### Example

The committee elected a new chairperson at its first meeting.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">委員會在第一次會議中選出新主席。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Chairperson</strong> 不區分性別，也常簡稱 <strong>chair</strong>。<strong>Chairperson of the board</strong> 是董事會主席；vice chairperson 是副主席。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="chariot">
<h2 id="chariot">chariot <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">古代戰車；雙輪馬車</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- ride in a chariot
- drive a chariot
- a horse-drawn / racing / golden chariot
- a chariot race

### Example

The museum displays a replica of a horse-drawn chariot.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">博物館展示了一輛馬拉戰車的複製品。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Ride in a chariot</strong> 是乘坐戰車；<strong>drive a chariot</strong> 是駕馭戰車。<strong>Chariot race</strong> 指古代戰車競賽。</p>

</section>

<section class="vocabulary-entry" aria-labelledby="checkout">
<h2 id="checkout">checkout <span class="vocabulary-pos">[n.; attributive]</span></h2>

**Meaning:** <span lang="zh-Hant">商店結帳處；結帳程序；旅館退房；設備檢查</span>

### Collocations & patterns

- at / go to / wait at the checkout
- self-checkout
- a checkout counter / line / clerk
- a final checkout of the equipment
- check out of a hotel
- check out a book

### Example

Please leave your key at the front desk when you check out.

<span class="reading-translation" lang="zh-Hant">退房時，請將鑰匙留在櫃檯。</span>

### Usage note

<p lang="zh-Hant"><strong>Checkout</strong> 一字作名詞或名詞前修飾語；動詞寫成兩字 <strong>check out</strong>。如 checkout time（退房時間）、check out of a hotel（辦理退房）、check out a library book（借出圖書館的書）。<strong>A final checkout</strong> 也可指設備的最後檢查，需依語境判斷。 <a href="https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/checkout">Dictionary: checkout</a> · <a href="https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/check-out">check out</a></p>

</section>
