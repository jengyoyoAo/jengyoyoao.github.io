A little about me

I’m a Mathematics undergraduate at National Cheng Kung University. Mathematics gives me a way to think carefully about problems; programming lets me try those ideas out. Research is one part of my life, alongside teaching, community activities, and the things I’m still curious enough to try.

My personal motto is simple: “Trying new things, learning as I go.” I enjoy stepping into unfamiliar areas, asking questions, and improving through practice. I don’t expect every experiment to work the first time; I value what the process teaches me.
